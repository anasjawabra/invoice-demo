01Data model & required fields

Every KPI below is derived from a single invoice-level record. These are the fields the calculation depends on — nothing else is read.

Note for BA A record can be a member of more than one exclusion reason at once (e.g. duplicate and under objection). The invoice is excluded from Net Invoiced exactly once regardless of how many reasons apply — but if the real system also reports an "exclusions by category" breakdown elsewhere, that invoice's value will legitimately appear in more than one category bucket there. Decide up front whether category-level exclusion totals are expected to sum back to the KPI strip's single Excluded Amount (in the prototype, they do sum, but only because reason-overlap happens to be zero across the sample data).

02Scope filters

All six tiles react to the same two scope controls. Filtering happens once, before any KPI is calculated — every tile below reads from that already-filtered set.

invoice_set = ALL_INVOICES
  .filter(date BETWEEN period_start AND period_end)
  .filter(entity == selected_entity OR selected_entity == "All")
Decision needed The prototype's period presets (Previous Month, Last 3/6 Months, Last Year) anchor "today" to the latest date found in the dataset, since it's a static demo snapshot. In production, anchor to the real system clock (CURRENT_DATE), not to MAX(invoice.date).

03KPI Definitions

1 · Total Invoicede.g. 29,518,000
Gross value of every invoice in scope — before any exclusion is applied.
Business definition

The full face value of all invoices issued within the selected period and entity, with no adjustments. This is the "before any judgment calls" number — every other tile is a slice or derivative of this one.

Formula 
Total Invoiced = SUM(invoice.amount) FOR invoice IN invoice_set

2 · Excluded Amounte.g. 10,357,500
Value stripped out before "net" is calculated — never counted as collected, never counted as outstanding either.
Business definition

The value of invoices that should not count toward the collection target at all, because collecting on them isn't a realistic or fair expectation right now. An invoice is excluded if any of the following is true:

  • Duplicate — status == 'duplicate'
  • Under appeal / dispute — hasOpenObjection == true
  • Invalid debtor — debtorInvalid == true (struck-off commercial registry or deceased debtor)
  • Referred to enforcement — collectedVia == 'enforcement'
Formula 
is_excluded(invoice) =
     invoice.status == 'duplicate'
  OR invoice.hasOpenObjection == true
  OR invoice.debtorInvalid == true
  OR invoice.collectedVia == 'enforcement'

Excluded Amount = SUM(invoice.amount)
  FOR invoice IN invoice_set WHERE is_excluded(invoice)

Confirm with the business owner whether this is the final exclusion policy for the real KPI, and whether any reason should itself be time-bound (e.g. an objection older than N days auto-reverts into Net Invoiced).
3 · Net Invoicede.g. 19,160,500
The denominator for Collection Rate — what the ministry can realistically expect to collect.
Business definition

Total Invoiced minus Excluded Amount. This is the base every downstream KPI (Collected, Collection Rate, Uncollected) is measured against.

Formula 
Net Invoiced = Total Invoiced − Excluded Amount

4 · Total Collectede.g. 14,306,500
Cash actually received, within the net-invoiced (non-excluded) population.
Business definition

The value of non-excluded invoices that have been fully paid and confirmed.

Formula 
Total Collected = SUM(invoice.amount)
  FOR invoice IN invoice_set
  WHERE NOT is_excluded(invoice)
    AND invoice.status == 'approved'
status == 'approved'is_excluded()
Edge case "Approved" is the prototype's single stand-in for "payment received." In the real payment lifecycle there may be intermediate states (e.g. payment submitted but not yet cleared) — confirm which real status value(s) should map to "collected" for this KPI, since counting too early would overstate cash actually in hand.
5 · Collection Ratee.g. 74.7%
The single headline performance number — Collected as a share of what was realistically invoiced.
Business definition

What fraction of the money the ministry could reasonably expect to collect has actually been collected. Deliberately measured against Net Invoiced, not Total Invoiced — so duplicate/disputed/enforcement-referred invoices don't unfairly drag the rate down.

Formula 
Collection Rate = ROUND(Total Collected / Net Invoiced × 100, 1)
  (0% if Net Invoiced == 0 — avoid divide-by-zero)
Total CollectedNet Invoiced
6 · Uncollectede.g. 4,854,000
Money still outstanding on invoices the ministry issued this year and hasn't excluded.
Business definition

Of this year's realistically-collectible invoices, how much is still sitting unpaid. Restricted to invoices issued in the current year — this reconciles accrual-basis invoicing (an invoice can be issued in one year and paid in the next) with a cash-basis "what's still owed to us right now" KPI, so multi-year carry-over debt doesn't silently inflate this specific tile.

Formula 
Uncollected = SUM(invoice.amount)
  FOR invoice IN invoice_set
  WHERE NOT is_excluded(invoice)
    AND invoice.status != 'approved'
    AND YEAR(invoice.date) == CURRENT_YEAR
status != 'approved'is_excluded()YEAR(date)
Edge case — arithmetic does not always close Because of the current-year restriction above, Total Collected + Uncollected is not guaranteed to equal Net Invoiced — a non-approved, non-excluded invoice from a prior year is inside Net Invoiced but outside both Collected and Uncollected. In the sample data the three numbers happen to tie out exactly because there's no such prior-year residue to test against — do not assume that will hold once real multi-year data is loaded. Decide whether prior-year outstanding balances need their own KPI tile (e.g. "Prior-Year Arrears") before this ships.

04Cross-cutting edge cases & open questions

1 · Zero-invoice period If the selected period/entity has no invoices at all, Net Invoiced is 0 and Collection Rate must short-circuit to 0% (not NaN or a divide-by-zero error). Confirm the UI should show "0%" rather than "—" / "N/A" in this state.
2 · Currency & rounding All six tiles are single-currency (SAR) in the prototype. If multi-currency invoices exist in production, confirm the FX conversion point (at invoice date? at report-run date?) before summing. Collection Rate is rounded to 1 decimal place — confirm that precision is acceptable for executive reporting versus, say, 2 decimals for audit exports.
3 · Real-time vs. batch Decide the refresh cadence for this strip in production — real-time query on page load, or a pre-aggregated nightly batch. The exclusion and collection-status fields (hasOpenObjection, debtorInvalid, status) likely come from different source systems in reality; the BA should confirm which system is authoritative for each and how often each syncs.
