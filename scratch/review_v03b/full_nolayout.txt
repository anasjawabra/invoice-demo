Executive Summary
Intelligent Invoice Management is a revenue and receivables intelligence solution for municipal
investment and lease revenues, collections and enforcement oversight. Existing platforms retain
transactional ownership; this solution maintains analytical mappings, evidence, forecasts and reports.
Tahseel remains authoritative for official collection figures, while other attributes follow the field-specific
authority matrix in Section 3.1.1.
The priority business outcome is a reliable link from Sanad non-fine enforcement records to their
underlying SADAD invoices. The platform checks structured references first and retrieves associated
documents only when links are unusable. It separates extracted numbers from validated relationships,
supports one case with several invoices and preserves unsupported cases as unresolved without
duplicating case values or invoice balances.
A governed semantic metrics service supports KPI and Performance Intelligence, forecasting and
dynamic conversational reporting. Users can create and refine editable reports, save definitions and
refresh or distribute them within approved permissions. Official KPI definitions, baselines, targets and
thresholds change only through authorized approval and versioning; historical commitments remain
reproducible.
Relevant Furas-originated investment/lease data is in scope. Procurement, supplier payments, tax
compliance, contract-award/amendment administration, direct payment processing and platform-executed
legal actions are excluded. Human review remains essential for ambiguous mappings, governance
changes and report distribution.
The prototype uses controlled sample data and simulated integrations. Production depends on ownerapproved source roles, field authority, formulas, access, validation data and security design. The supplied
files do not establish live integration availability, an approved KPI catalog or completed stage-gate
approval.
Business benefits will be measured as released employee capacity, collection outcomes attributable to
the solution and adoption. Previous revenue-accuracy and GDP-linked growth claims are not accepted
commitments. Section 5 defines measurement and approval requirements; Section 6 retains the original
milestone dates as proposed dates subject to readiness and re-baselining.

2 Business Context
2.1 Business Problem
Revenue information is distributed across Tahseel, Makeen, Sanad and other source platforms.
Inconsistent identifiers and fragmented access make reconciliation, investment-revenue analysis and
enforcement monitoring difficult. A Sanad enforcement reference may lack a usable structured link to one
or more SADAD invoices, limiting reliable portfolio reporting.
The solution addresses intake and normalization, evidence-backed validation, anomaly review, case
routing and governed analytics. Descriptions of current platform capabilities and manual cycle times in the
source are working assumptions, not verified platform assessments.

3.1.2 Semantic and Business Metrics Contract
Governed definition

Required approved content and release rule

Entity grains and keys

Invoice fact: unique source-native invoice ID plus approved SADAD crosswalk;
collection fact: unique collection transaction/event with reversals; contract: originating
contract ID/version; enforcement case: Sanad record ID and separate Board of
Grievances reference. Preserve unresolved identities.

Relationships and
allocation

Use a versioned case–invoice bridge carrying evidence and validation status. Sum
invoice balances once per invoice and case amounts once per case. Aggregate each
grain before combining views. If a source later links one invoice to multiple cases,
prevent repeated balances across those cases. Allocate case amounts only under an
approved method/version; otherwise show unallocated amounts separately.

Net invoicing

Define NI(P,C,v) = SUM of owner-approved signed invoice events/adjustments included
by rule version v for period P and population C. Approval must specify included
statuses, cancellations, credit notes, reversals, exclusions, relevant date, currency and
tax treatment. This is a configuration contract, not a claim that the Ministry’s formula has
been supplied.

Collection rate

Define CR(P,C,v) = 100 × N(P,C,v) / D(P,C,v), only when the approved denominator is
nonzero. Owner must specify numerator event/status and reversal treatment,
denominator (including whether approved net invoicing or another eligible receivable
base), exclusions, cohort, calendar and date alignment. Zero/missing denominator
returns “not applicable”/“unavailable”, not an invented percentage.

Time and status
semantics

Distinguish invoice issue date, due date, collection event/value date, source update
time, ingestion time, enforcement referral date and snapshot as-of date. Maintain
source-to-business status mappings. Report source cutoff and organizational/revenueclassification versions.

KPI catalog contract

Each KPI stores ID/name, rationale, formula/version, unit, owner, measurement period,
dimensions, aggregation/exclusion rules, baseline period/population/method/value,
target, intended direction/range, threshold, approver and effective dates. Proposed
records cannot silently become official.

Lineage and
authorization

Every result links to source snapshot and governed measure version. Scope queries
and evidence by owner-approved role/row/column policies. Preserve historical
commitments and govern any restated historical series separately.

Release gate

The actual approved net-invoicing and collection-rate methods are not included in the
two supplied files. Prototype formulas must be marked illustrative; production official KPI
use is blocked until the business owner approves populated definitions and
reconciliation examples.

Reporting partitions must be explicit: “referred” invoices are distinct invoices with at least one validated,
relevant case relationship; “not referred” requires adequate confirmed referral coverage; unknown or
unresolved referral status is separate. Collections on referred invoices are not automatically collections
attributable to enforcement. Use an owner-approved timing/attribution rule for that measure.

3.1.3 Conversational Reporting Technical Approach
Proposed approach: an Arabic conversational interface produces a schema-validated report specification
containing approved measure IDs/versions, dimensions, filters, period, comparisons, calculations and
visualization layout. A policy service validates scope and allowed operations. A semantic query service
compiles parameterized read-only queries; a report service renders an editable report and stores its
versioned definition. The language model has no direct source-database credentials and cannot invent
production formulas.

Refinements modify the report specification and repeat validation. Saved definitions pin measure
versions; scheduled refresh rechecks the owner and each recipient, identifies incompatible/retired
definitions and requires controlled migration. PDF and XLSX outputs carry source, period, filters,

Power BI describes the intended analyst-like experience; it is not a prescribed product dependency. OCI

5.4 Capability Acceptance Tests
The following are proposed acceptance criteria for owner agreement, not test results. Before evaluation,
approve a representative, access-controlled gold dataset with sufficient cases per risk stratum and
independent adjudication. Record sample sizes, prevalence, confidence intervals and coverage; keep
model-tuning data separate from held-out acceptance data. Report precision as correct accepted items
divided by accepted items, and recall as recovered gold items divided by eligible gold items.
Prototype checks demonstrate behavior with samples and seeded exceptions; they cannot establish
production accuracy. Alpha verifies components; Beta evaluates approved integrations, formulas and
representative data. Production release requires signed acceptance, resolved critical defects and the
gates in Section 6.3. Exact deterministic controls use 100% pass criteria within the agreed suite, not
claims of universal error-free operation.

AC-01 SADAD extraction
Requirements: FR-001/002/004/016. Propose ≥99% exact-token precision and ≥95% recall across all
candidate SADAD numbers, with leading zeros preserved; report Arabic/English, text/scanned and multiinvoice strata separately.
Validation evidence: Gold-labeled documents with correct page locations; include multiple numbers, nonSADAD identifiers, missing/illegible scans and structured-reference bypass cases.

AC-02 Validated relationships
Requirements: FR-005/016. Propose ≥99% precision for accepted links and ≥95% recall on resolvable
gold links; report automated and human-confirmed results separately. Require zero unsupported autoacceptances in the negative test suite.
Validation evidence: Independent owner adjudication of case–invoice links; include a valid-looking
number belonging to a different case, conflicting sources and debtor/amount-only candidates.

AC-03 One-to-many and unresolved cases

unresolved/partial status; no inferred invoice numbers, duplicate balances, repeated case values or
unapproved equal allocations.
Validation evidence: One case with several invoices; duplicate documents; partial mapping; no evidence;
one invoice linked to multiple cases; unallocated case amount. Reconcile totals to hand-checked gold
results.

AC-04 Mapping evidence
Requirements: FR-002/012/016. 100% of mapping attempts have original/normalized candidate, source
record/version, document/page where applicable, validation result, extraction confidence, relationship
assessment and review history.
Validation evidence: Inspect structured and OCR attempts, rejected links and reprocessed documents;

reversals, period alignment, investment filters, Amanah comparison and enforcement partitions; verify
export metadata.
