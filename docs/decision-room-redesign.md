# «غرفة القرارات» / Decision Room — redesign requirements and the item contract (6 Nov 2026)

## Requirements (from the user)
1. **Remove user assignment** everywhere (Decision Room and its Dashboard preview): assign-to-user, responsible-person selectors/filters, «غير مُسند/بلا مسؤول» labels, assignment-based deadlines and overdue-task KPIs, anything implying task management. Do NOT replace it with another ownership workflow. Simple follow-up model only: **وضع علامة للمتابعة** (flag for follow-up) · **تمت المتابعة** (follow-up completed) · **إزالة علامة المتابعة** (remove flag) · optional note. Record who changed the flag and when (audit) without treating that person as an assignee. «Follow-up completed» = someone reviewed / followed up; it NEVER means the issue is resolved, an invoice paid or a recommendation approved. The underlying issue's current condition stays separate from its flag. Existing notes and history are preserved on migration.
2. **Each item answers a decision question**: what happened · which Amanah / municipality / revenue source · the amount or gap · the comparison basis · the supporting evidence · what to review or consider next. Keep separate: **verified facts · calculated deviations · unknown causes · proposed recommendations** (collection below plan is a calculated deviation; its cause is not «proven»). Never recommend lowering a target just because actual is below it.
3. **Consolidate categories** (generate an item only where the data supports it): collection performance below an approved target · material overdue balances and aging · revenue-source deterioration · investment installments due without a verified invoice link · unresolved enforcement/invoice relationships · material data conflicts affecting financial interpretation. Distinguish a **management decision**, an **operational review** and a **data clarification**; not every alert is a decision needing approval; no duplicates.
4. **Page structure**: compact title + purpose → ONE shared filter toolbar (primary: Period, Amanah/entity, Category; More: revenue source, priority, follow-up flag; no duplicate entity/source filters) → small summary strip (open financial issues · items flagged for follow-up · items needing evidence verification; a financial total ONLY when the populations are additive and deduplicated — never sum overlapping overdue, target gaps and forecast shortfalls) → 2–3 focused charts → ranked items → expandable evidence + follow-up history.
5. **Charts** (only what the data supports; no decoration, no repeat of the dashboard): ranked horizontal bars of largest overdue exposure by Amanah or revenue source · actual vs planned collection (bullet / grouped bars) ONLY where an approved comparable plan exists · aging distribution of overdue balances · a trend inside the item details. Chart selections filter the items or open supporting records; label units, period, cutoff, basis; unavailable ≠ zero.
6. **Item summaries and details**: compact row = clear business title · category + affected entity · priority label **High / Medium / Low with a transparent explanation (replace scores like 0.82)** · the relevant amount explicitly labelled (exposure / gap / …) · follow-up flag · «عرض التحليل». Expanded: short finding · relevant metrics · one useful chart where appropriate · evidence + supporting invoice links · missing information · recommended next step or decision OPTIONS with implications · follow-up controls and history. No repeating the same recommendation under reason / recommendation / required decision.
7. **Recommendation quality**: specific to the evidence (review highest-value overdue invoices; check a recorded objection's status; verify a proposed invoice/order link; reconcile conflicting amounts first; review investment installments against confirmed Furas contract records; investigate a plan deviation before considering corrective measures). No invented causes, recovery probabilities, owners or deadlines; exposure is never «expected recovered revenue». Proposed ≠ formally approved; flagging / completing follow-up never executes enforcement, changes targets or alters financial records.
8. **Financial accuracy**: reuse approved calculations, composite invoice identity, confirmed relationships. Distinguish unpaid vs overdue; excluded amounts vs payment status; invoice-period collection vs payments received in the period; annual target vs planned-to-date; observed vs forecast. Planned-to-date series only when supported; illustrative forecast assumptions labelled. Overlapping items disclose the overlap.
9. **Dashboard preview** (`/insights`, `DecisionSummary`) uses the same item model: compact highest-priority findings (title · labelled amount · priority · follow-up flag · link); no assignment or overdue-assigned-action metrics.
10. Validate: filtering and chart interactions · flag / unflag / completed · notes and audit history persistence · an issue that stays financially open after follow-up · no duplicates or misleading totals · invoice/contract/order links · dashboard consistency · permissions · ar/en · mobile.

## Item contract (what the data layer returns; the UI codes against it)
```
Item = {
  key,                                   // STABLE (the store is keyed by it)
  kind: 'decision' | 'review' | 'clarification',          // management decision · operational review · data clarification
  category: 'plan_deviation' | 'overdue_aging' | 'source_deterioration' | 'installments_unlinked' | 'enforcement_links' | 'data_conflicts',
  title{ar,en}, question{ar,en},         // business title + the decision/review question
  what{ar,en},                           // what happened (one or two sentences)
  affected:{ amanah?, municipality?, source?, label{ar,en} },
  period:{from,to}, cutoff,
  measure:{ kind:'exposure'|'gap'|'overdue'|'amount'|'count', label{ar,en}, amount|count, basis{ar,en} },   // THE labelled amount of the row
  comparison:{ basis{ar,en}, values:[{label{ar,en}, value, unit}] },                                       // comparison basis stated
  facts:[ {ar,en, evidence?} ],          // verified source facts
  calculated:[ {label{ar,en}, value, unit, formula{ar,en}?} ],      // calculated deviations
  unknown:[ {ar,en} ],                   // unknown causes (never stated as proven)
  proposed:[ {ar,en} ],                  // proposed review steps (NOT approved decisions)
  options:[ {title{ar,en}, implication{ar,en}} ],                    // only when several responses are reasonable
  evidence:[ {label{ar,en}, to, count?, status:'source_record'|'calculated'|'unverified'|'missing'} ],   // links to invoices/contracts/orders with scope kept
  missing:[ {ar,en} ],
  priority:{ level:'high'|'medium'|'low', why{ar,en}, factors:[{label{ar,en}, value}] },                  // rule-based, thresholds shown; no scores
  verification:'verified'|'needs_verification',                      // does the evidence still need checking?
  chart?: { block }                      // optional per-item chart block (shared chart spec), e.g. trend
  overlap:[ {with:key, note{ar,en}} ],   // disclosed overlap with other items
  additive:boolean,                      // included in the summary's financial total (deduplicated population)?
  condition:{ open:boolean, since? }     // the underlying issue's current condition (from the data), independent of the follow-up flag
}
FollowUp (store, per key) = { flag:'none'|'flagged'|'followed_up', note?, history:[{ at, by, action:'flag'|'unflag'|'followed_up'|'note'|'migrated', note? }] }   // no assignee / owner / due
```

## Implemented data model (data layer, 6 Nov 2026)

Files: `src/data/decisionRoom.js` (items, priority, overlap, summary, filters) · `decisionCharts.js` (page charts + item trend) · `decisionStore.js` (follow-up store + migration) · `useDecisions.js` (hook). Tests: `npm run test:decisions` (items, charts), `npm run test:followup` (store, migration).
The contract above is implemented as written; the **additions and exact semantics** are below. Units in `calculated[].unit` / `comparison.values[].unit`: `SAR` (money, whole riyals) · `pct` (a FRACTION 0..1, format ×100) · `pp` (percentage points, a plain number) · `count` · `days`. Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### Items — `buildDecisionItems(inp) → { items, context }`
- Ranked High → Medium → Low, then by the measure amount (descending), then by key. `context = { totalOverdue, totalNotDue, uncollected, net, agedTotal, cutoff, period }` (scope totals the strip may quote).
- Additions to the contract: `periodBasis: 'selected' | 'fiscal_year' | 'standing'` (plan items use the fiscal year to the reporting date; orders and installments are `standing`, with `period.from = null`) · `trend: boolean` (a monthly trend can be drawn — call `trend(item)`, see below) · `priority.capped` · every `priority.factors[i]` = `{ label, value, unit, text{ar,en}, grade:'high'|'medium'|'low', threshold{ar,en} }` · `facts[i].evidence` is a status string (`source_record | calculated | unverified`), not a link · `measure.count` OR `measure.amount` (never both) · `comparison.values[i] = { label, value, unit }`.
- Keys (stable; the store is keyed by them): `plan:ministry` · `plan:<amanah>` · `overdue:amanah:<amanah>` · `overdue:source:<source>` · `source:<source>` · `contracts:unlinked` · `contracts:no_contract` · `orders:suspended` · `orders:links` · `data:amount_conflicts` · `data:missing_fields`.
- What is generated, and only when the data supports it:
  - **plan_deviation** (kind `decision`): collected (payment-date basis) is ≥ 10 % below the amount **planned to date** — the Ministry-level item only when no Amanah filter narrows the scope, then up to 4 entities (largest gap). The annual target is stated separately in `comparison` (label «shown separately»), never as the basis. Not generated when a source filter is set (the plan is per entity). The year-end estimate appears only as a labelled illustrative `calculated` entry. Options: investigate causes first / review the entity's collection actions once causes are known (any plan or target change belongs to the planning process, nothing is executed here). Never a recommendation to lower a target.
  - **overdue_aging**: one item per Amanah holding ≥ 5 % of the scope's overdue (or ≥ 10 % of its balance older than 180 days), max 6; measure = period overdue (additive) or, when only the aged part is material, the standing balance older than 180 days. `kind:'decision'` (with 3 `options`) when the Amanah holds ≥ 10 % of the scope's 180+ balance (a route choice exists), else `review`. Plus up to 3 **by-source** items (≥ 20 % of the overdue; only when several sources are in scope).
  - **source_deterioration** (`review`): a source whose collection rate fell ≥ 5 points against the **previous equivalent period** (same scope, each period measured at its own end — `rev.prevSnapshot` + `rev.comparison.comparable`), source ≥ 3 % of net; nothing without a comparable previous period.
  - **installments_unlinked**: `contracts:unlinked` (`review`, `needs_verification`; proposed-rule label kept; arrears appear only as «for context only»); `contracts:no_contract` (`clarification`). Arrears and future-not-invoiced are no longer separate items.
  - **enforcement_links** (`review`): suspended orders; unresolved links (proposed links + unresolved references; `needs_verification`).
  - **data_conflicts** (`clarification`, `needs_verification`): amount conflicts; mandatory fields missing.
- `unknown` is always present (the cause is never stated as proven); an objection is the only documented reason and is reported at scope level when it exists. `options` only on plan items and on aged-debt decision items, each with an implication. No owners, deadlines, recovery probability or «expected recovered revenue» anywhere (a regex list in the test enforces it, EN and AR).
- Evidence links: list links keep the scope (`/collection?amanah=…&src=…&category=overdue&from=…&to=…`), invoices `invoicePath(id)` (ids from the snapshot's quality lists), orders `orderPath(enforceNum)`, `/planning`, `/contracts`, `/enforcement?view=orders`.

### Priority thresholds (`THRESHOLDS`, `PRIORITY_RULE` in decisionRoom.js)
Level = **High** when every factor (at most two) is High · **Medium** when one factor is High or two are Medium · **Low** otherwise · an item with `verification:'needs_verification'` is held at Medium (`priority.capped`). No score.
| Factor | High | Medium |
|---|---|---|
| Share of the scope's overdue (or of its 180+ balance) | ≥ 25 % | ≥ 10 % |
| Longest overdue (days; a 180+ balance counts as 181) | > 180 | > 90 |
| Plan gap ÷ planned to date | ≥ 25 % | ≥ 10 % |
| Plan gap ÷ annual target | ≥ 10 % | ≥ 3 % |
| Fall in collection rate (points) | ≥ 15 | ≥ 5 |
| Installment / order / conflict value ÷ net invoiced | ≥ 5 % / 5 % / 2 % | ≥ 1 % / 1 % / 0.5 % |
| Counts: installments / orders / records / missing fields | ≥ 20 / 10 / 20 / 50 | ≥ 5 / 3 / 5 / 10 |

### Overlap, additivity, summary
- `overlap[{with, note}]` is symmetric. Disclosed: Amanah ↔ source ↔ source-deterioration items (same unpaid invoices), plan gap ↔ overdue of the same entity (different basis), suspended orders ↔ overdue, data items ↔ invoice items. Installments of the generated schedule have no invoice: no overlap.
- `additive:true` only for `overdue:amanah:*` items whose measure is the period overdue (the Amanah cells partition the scope: disjoint).
- `summaryOf(items, context?) → { total, open, openFinancialIssues, flagged, followedUp, needsVerification, financialTotal, financialTotalReason, overlapNote }` — computed on the items it is given (pass the filtered list to make the strip follow the filters). `openFinancialIssues` = open items whose measure is money (`exposure|gap|overdue|amount`); `flagged` = flag `flagged`; `needsVerification` = open items with `needs_verification`. `financialTotal = { amount, label, basis, itemKeys, scopeTotal } | null` — the sum of the additive items, **null with `financialTotalReason`** when none is additive or two additive items overlap; `overlapNote` is set whenever any item overlaps. A followed-up item whose issue is open still counts as open.

### Follow-up store — `decisionStore.js` (key `ib_decision_followup_v2`)
`{ v:2, items:{ [key]:{ flag:'none'|'flagged'|'followed_up', note, history:[{at, by, action:'flag'|'followed_up'|'unflag'|'note'|'migrated', note?, i18n?}] } } }`. Pure functions returning `{store}` or `{error}`: `flag(store,key,{note},ctx)` (none/followed_up → flagged; `already_flagged`) · `complete(...)` (only from flagged; `not_flagged`) · `unflag(...)` (flagged/followed_up → none) · `addNote(store,key,text,ctx)` (`text_required`); `ctx = {by, at}` is required (`actor_required`) and is audit only. `followUpOf(store,key)`, `loadStore`, `saveStore`, `migrateV1`, `FLAGS`, `ACTIONS` (labels). Removed: assign, transition, ROLES, STATUSES, ALLOWED, REASON_REQUIRED, dueDateOf, deferred/rejected.
- **Migration** (once, in `loadStore` when v2 is absent; v1 stays in storage untouched): new → none · in_progress → flagged · completed → followed_up · deferred / rejected / resolved → none. Notes and history are kept in order; v1 `status` events and the explanatory entry per item become `migrated` entries (`note` in English, `i18n` {ar,en}; show `i18n` when present); v1 `assign` events become a neutral `migrated` entry (no person, owner, role or due date is carried; `by` stays as the actor of the event). Owner / assignee / due / meta are not carried.
- Items from `withState(items, store)` gain `followUp = { flag, note, history, lastBy, lastAt }`; `condition` is never touched.

### Charts — `buildDecisionCharts(inp, lang) → { overdueRank:{default, amanah, source}, planVsActual, aging, unavailable }`
Same `inp` as the items. Shared-spec blocks (`bar`/`hbar`) with extra fields `unit:'SAR'`, `period`, `cutoff`, `basis` (text), `note` (unit, period, cutoff, basis) and `marks[i] = { label, filter }` aligned with `labels[i]`, where `filter` is `{ amanah } | { source } | { category, amanah? } | { category }` — apply it with `filterItems(items, filter)`. `overdueRank.amanah` is the top 8 (period overdue, largest first); `overdueRank.source` is null when only one source is in scope. `planVsActual` is a grouped `bar` (Planned to date vs Collected, by entity) only when an approved comparable plan exists, else **null** and `unavailable.planVsActual` says why. `aging` = overdue buckets 1–30 … 180+ of the standing balance (not-yet-due left out); null + reason if unavailable. `itemTrend(item, {snapshot, lang}) → block | null` (net invoiced vs collected by invoice month for the item's Amanah/source; only for `overdue_aging` and `source_deterioration` items with ≥ 2 months; `item.trend` tells whether it exists).

### Hook — `useDecisions()`
`{ ready, loading, error, items, summary, charts, context, trend(item), labels, canReview, by, today, flag(it,{note}), complete(it,{note}), unflag(it,{note}), note(it,text), followUp(it) }`. Each mutator returns `{ok}` or `{ok:false, error}` (`no_permission` unless `rev.canReview`). Items come from the organisation-scoped snapshot, cases and plan, and `allowedAmanahs` filters again. `filterItems(items, {category, amanah, source, kind, priority, flag})` is exported from decisionRoom.js; an item without an Amanah (Ministry plan, scope-wide data items) does not match an Amanah filter.

**Page order.** compact title + purpose (+ the existing link to the non-collection analysis) → ONE shared toolbar (`ScopeFilters`): primary **Period · Amanah/entity · Category**; «المزيد من الفلاتر»: **revenue source · priority · follow-up flag** (on mobile everything is in the Filter drawer). Period / Amanah / source are the shared revenue scope; category / priority / flag (and the open item, `?item=`) live in the URL (`?category=&priority=&flag=&item=`). The date-basis line states that the period applies to the invoice issue date while plan items use the fiscal year to the reporting date and installments / orders are standing balances; every item restates its own basis. → small **strip**: open financial issues · flagged for follow-up · evidence to verify (+ the financial total only when `summary.financialTotal` is non-null, with label, basis, share of the scope overdue and the overlap note; nothing at all otherwise). The strip is computed on the FILTERED list (`summaryOf(view)`), so it reconciles with the count beside the toolbar → **2–3 charts** (`ReportChart` with `onSelect`; overdue exposure ranked, with the by-entity / by-source switch; collected against the plan to date only when `charts.planVsActual` exists; overdue aging). A bar sets the list filter from `marks[i].filter` and shows the chip «من الرسم: … ×»; choosing it again clears it; each chart carries unit / period / cutoff / basis in its note and a table alternative; unavailable charts are shown as «غير متاح — <reason>», never as zero → ranked list → expandable analysis.

**Row** (compact): business title · category + affected entity · kind badge (قرار إداري / مراجعة تشغيلية / توضيح بيانات) · priority label with «لماذا؟» (factors, grade, thresholds, the rule; no score) · the labelled amount («الفجوة عن المخطط حتى تاريخه 9.14 مليار ريال») · follow-up chip (and, once flagged or followed up, the separate financial-condition chip «مفتوحة ماليًا») · «عرض التحليل» (`aria-expanded`). «يحتاج تحقق الدليل» chip when `verification === 'needs_verification'`. No lanes, owner, due date or assignment anywhere.

**Expanded analysis (one place, no repetition).** the question + the finding · metrics with the measurement period, cutoff, amount basis and comparison basis · four separate blocks — **حقائق موثقة / فروقات محسوبة / أسباب غير معروفة / مقترحات للمراجعة** (the last one labelled «مقترح — ليس قراراً معتمداً», as are the decision options with their implications) · ONE chart (`trend(item)` when it returns a block) · evidence with source-record / calculated / unverified / missing status and links (`RecordLink`: opens the ordinary invoice / order / contract / list page and «← Back to Decision room» returns to the same item and scroll position) · missing information · the disclosed overlap («لا تُجمع المبالغ») · the **follow-up panel**.

**Follow-up.** Buttons «وضع علامة للمتابعة» (from none / followed up) · «تمت المتابعة» (only from flagged) · «إزالة علامة المتابعة» (flagged / followed up) + an optional note and «إضافة ملاحظة فقط». The persistent statement next to the controls: «تمت المتابعة تعني أنه رُوجع أو تمت متابعته؛ لا تعني أن المشكلة المالية حُلّت أو أن الفاتورة سُدّدت أو أن التوصية اعتُمدت. العلامة لا تنفّذ إجراءً تنفيذياً ولا تغيّر مستهدفاً ولا تعدّل أي سجل.» The flag and the financial condition («مفتوحة ماليًا», from the data, with `since` when present) are shown side by side but separately: a followed-up item stays financially open and still counts as an open financial issue in the strip. History = time (Riyadh), action, «سُجّل بواسطة <name>» (audit only; nobody is «responsible»), note; migrated entries show their bilingual `i18n` text. Users without `rev.canReview` (`user.canReview === false`, e.g. the demo `auditor` account) see the available buttons DISABLED with the reason as a visible line and a tooltip.

**Dashboard preview** (`DecisionSummary`, same place in `InsightsDashboard`, no edit needed there): «أبرز ما يحتاج مراجعة» — three counts in one line, then the 4 highest-priority OPEN findings of the same ranked model (title · category · entity · priority · labelled amount · flag chip · «عرض التحليل» → `/decisions?item=<key>`, which opens and scrolls to that item). No assignment, no overdue-assigned-action metric.

**States.** loading skeleton / error with retry (`AsyncBlock`); no results → `NoResults` with reset (clears the page filters, the chart chip and the shared scope; nothing is widened silently); a deep link to an item hidden by a filter shows a notice with «مسح تصفيات الصفحة»; empty scope → «لا بنود في هذا النطاق»; RTL / LTR; rows are buttons with `aria-expanded`; 375 px: no horizontal overflow (checked `scrollWidth === innerWidth`).
