# Investment contracts — lifecycle page (Phase 1)

Branch `feat/contracts-lifecycle-phase1` (from `main` at dd2d107). Approved plan: `IIM-Requirements-Handover/02_Investment_Contracts/Investment_Contracts_Review_and_Implementation_Plan` (D1–D21, gaps G1–G20, phases).
Everything here runs on the **synthetic** demo data (every value on the contract pages is illustrative: see «Group A corrections» below); no Furas, Tahseel or SADAD meaning is asserted that the evidence does not support.

## What changed

**Contract page (`/contracts/:no`)** — the main section is now «الدفعات والفواتير — من الإنشاء إلى الانتهاء»: one row per scheduled installment for the WHOLE contract (first installment → contract end), cut by segments (all · paid · invoiced and due · upcoming · excluded/cancelled · before the data range). Each row shows installment no. «n of N» and year, due date, invoice id + SADAD number, invoice issue date, amount, collected, remaining, paid-in-full / last-payment date and state; invoiced rows expand to their payment events (date, amount, channel, running total, days against the due date). A legend explains every state. Orders show opened date and «closed: not recorded». The figures strip is now gross invoiced · exclusions · net invoiced · collected · uncollected · collection rate on net invoiced, with coverage («n of N installments have data»).

**Contracts list (`/contracts`)** — new tile, filter and column for «due — no invoice linked» (a CANDIDATE rule, pending confirmation), the rate column is the governed collection rate on net invoiced (collected ÷ net; «not available» when net is zero), loading/error states, the illustrative-data badge and a scope line (the period selector does not apply to contract schedules).

**Decision Room** — new item `contracts:unlinked`; `contracts:no_contract` now states the period basis it really uses.

**Invoice page** — beneficiary mobile removed (also from the API), beneficiary id masked.

**Search / numbers** — contracts are found by exact (trimmed, case-folded) match with a known number (`contractByNumber`); the generator emits `CT-yyyy-nnnn`, `CN-nnnnn`, `ECN-nnnnn`, `EDROCN-nnnnn`; free-text extraction also reads the `CN-/ECN-/EDROCN-` forms.

## Rules (see the review for the evidence)
* Net invoiced = gross invoiced − exclusions; uncollected = net invoiced − collected; collection rate on net = collected ÷ net; zero or unknown denominator → «not calculable». «Collected of what is due to date» is a different figure and is labelled so.
* Three kinds of empty cell: **zero** (a confirmed 0), **not applicable yet** («—», an upcoming installment has no invoice) and **not available from source** (before the data range, or no invoice linked). Unknown is never summed as zero; totals print coverage.
* No date is derived as «due − 10» in the contract card: an invoice date is the invoice's own issue date, otherwise «not invoiced». (The generator still creates synthetic invoices 10 days before due — that is data creation, not display logic.)
* «Term ended on <date>» is a date fact kept apart from the contract status; no status is inferred from dates.
* An order's closure or referral changes no amount: contract totals are read only from invoices and payment events.
* The schedule (`ct.sched`) is built without any random draw. `ct.dues` (the invoicing window) and every generated invoice/payment/request are byte-identical to `main` (hash of the ledger identical on the compact and full worlds).

## Deviation from the plan
G4 proposed splitting «unlinked» into «no invoice» and «invoice not linked to this contract». The second needs the generator's hidden truth about unmatched invoices, which a real system cannot know, so the card shows one honest state — «Due — no invoice linked to this contract» — and says an unmatched invoice may exist elsewhere.

## Lateness — one rule (`src/data/lateness.js`)
Two questions, never mixed. **Overdue unpaid days** = max(0, reference date − (due date + grace days)), reported only while an amount is still owed and the invoice is neither excluded nor cancelled (contract card: `null` otherwise; invoice list: 0). The invoice page's payment panel no longer recomputes it: it takes the engine's value (grace included). **Payment lateness** describes a payment that was made: `offset` = payment date − due date and `lateBy` = days beyond the grace period (0 = on time); each payment event carries both. The grace period is the one global parameter (`cfg.graceDays`, default 0).

## Balance and source status — exactly what exists
* **Displayed amounts:** the «Collected (from the invoice)» and «Remaining (from the invoice)» columns are read from the installment's linked invoice and its payment events in the demo ledger — generated, illustrative data. In production the authoritative collection source is Tahseel (SADAD supports settlement analysis). No Furas field is used for them.
* **Scheduled contract balance:** NOT displayed and NOT returned. The generator still computes it internally; the API field `remainingContractBalance` was removed from the card. No Databank field supports a displayed balance: BALANCEAMOUNT is unconfirmed (a scheduled running balance in the report, fits 2 of 20 raw rows), BATCHAMOUNTBALANCE (the unpaid amount) is empty in the sample. Review proposal D4 stays deferred.
  *Superseded (historical evidence kept above):* the «fits 2 of 20 raw rows» observation came from a 20-row raw sample. The six Databank exports of 2 Nov 2026 show BALANCEAMOUNT is filled throughout and behaves like a running balance in most contracts, but its definition is still not given, so the decision is unchanged: the balance stays undisplayed. See [docs/furas-export-reassessment.md](furas-export-reassessment.md).
* **Furas source status:** NOT implemented — there is no column and no field. It depends on Data Bank (value list of BATCHSTATUS; three vocabularies exist; 7 of 10 raw «Paid» rows show no payment evidence). The page shows only the DERIVED installment state. The contract status is the source's own value (shown verbatim; unknown values marked «unrecognised»; empty → «Not provided by source»).
  *Superseded (historical evidence kept above):* «7 of 10 raw «Paid» rows show no payment evidence» and «three vocabularies exist» came from a 20-row raw sample. In the larger exports almost every «Paid» row carries a payment date, and the status values are one list per column that still has no approved meaning, so source status stays undisplayed and no status mapping is validated. See [docs/furas-export-reassessment.md](furas-export-reassessment.md).

## Group A corrections (after the six-export reassessment)
Local commit on this branch; plan in `IIM-Requirements-Handover/02_Investment_Contracts/Furas_v2_Review_and_Plan`. Nothing in Group B, no business rule and no financial figure was changed.

A displayed value is «Illustrative» (generated) or «From source». «From source» needs the card to name the source record the value was read from (`origin.sourceRecord`) and list the field (`origin.sourceBacked`); a verified schema mapping alone does not qualify. The demo loads no source record, so every contract value is illustrative. The contract page shows a note, a tag on each fact and a tag on each section; the list shows the note; the API card and roll-up carry `origin`.
2. **«Due — no invoice linked» is a candidate rule** over the generated demo ledger — never a finding from a Furas extract. Prerequisites: a complete validated schedule, a reliable contract identity, validated invoice linkage, an agreed due-date and grace rule. Applied to the contracts list (tile, filter, column, note), the contract page (state label, legend, note), the Decision Room item `contracts:unlinked`, the data dictionary and the API (`candidateRules` at that commit; renamed `proposedRules` in the data-accuracy pass — no consumer read the old name). No report or export carries the rule.
3. **Contract-number recognition** — the extraction pattern now reads the family `<letters>CN-<digits>` (CN, ECN, DROCN, DRRCN, EDROCN) plus the existing `CT-yyyy-nnnn`, `CNT-` and `CO-` forms; no closed prefix list. A number is looked up exactly (trimmed, case-folded). A found number establishes existence only: never a unique record, never an approved enforcement link.

## Data-accuracy corrections (demo vs the schema and the six exports)
The demo now says only what the verified evidence supports; it generates data but never presents it as source data. Nothing else was changed (no generator change: invoices, payments, orders and every contract total except «due to date» are identical).

* **Wording** (`src/data/contractRules.js`): unsupported information reads «Not available in the current data» (it replaces «Not available from source» / «Not provided by source»); an unconfirmed rule reads «Proposed rule — requires confirmation» (it replaces «candidate rule»). A field that exists in the schema is not treated as available data.
* **Proposed rules, each tagged where it is applied:** «due — no invoice linked», «term ended» (end date earlier than the reference date; the end date is missing for many contracts) and «overdue with no grace period» (the grace period is a business decision).
* **Calculation:** «net invoiced due to date» = net (gross − exclusions − cancelled, the base of the collection rate) of the INVOICED installments whose due date has come, before the collections matched to them (`netDueToDate`); «collected of what is due to date» = collected of those installments ÷ that net; `netDueToDate − collectedOfDue = remainingOfDue`. Excluded and cancelled invoices add nothing beyond what was collected, installments with no linked invoice (collection unknown, never a zero) and future installments are outside it. Earlier the figure summed scheduled installment amounts, including excluded and cancelled invoices and unlinked installments. The Product page tile «Receivables to date» sums the same amount before collections, so it is renamed «Net invoiced and due to date (before collections)».
* **Labels:** «Contract status (source)» → «(illustrative value)»; «Revenue item» → «(illustrative classification)» (the source has a service type with six values whose meanings are not confirmed); «Payment interval» → «(illustrative)» (in the limited sample payments are mostly annual and monthly was not observed — an observation, not a rule).
* **Limits panel** (`DATA_LIMITS`): a collapsible «What the current data supports — and what it does not» on the contracts list and the contract page; qualitative, no real figure. Kinds: not available · proposed rule · limited sample · illustrative.
* **Decision Room:** overdue item names the no-grace rule and the generated schedule; the upcoming-installments cause is a hypothesis (complete schedules are not available in the current data); the no-contract item says the match is by number alone and a missing match is not proof.
* **Not changed on purpose:** the generator's monthly/quarterly/semi-annual/annual intervals (changing them would change generated invoices), Group B items, and every business rule — all still require confirmation.

## Verification scope (read this before trusting «export parity»)
`npm run verify:exports` checks the EXISTING invoice outputs: the nine report models in three formats (docx, xlsx, pptx) against the invoice figures. There is no contract report and no contract export, so it does **not** validate any contract figure. Contract figures are covered by the platform tests: identities, the CSV reconciliation per contract (invoice export of the same contract), the roll-up equals the sum of the cards, net-due consistency and scope/cache behaviour.
